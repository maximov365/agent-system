---
name: agent-system-design-review
description: Critique and polish an implemented interface against its design intent in an agent-system project. Use for requested design review or a substantial visual acceptance pass.
---

Resolve the screen/state and baseline. View the running result and affected
interactions; use recordings for motion. Read [visual acceptance](../../../docs/VISUAL_QUALITY.md)
and the relevant [craft section](../../../docs/DESIGN_CRAFT.md), not the full role library.

Assess task clarity, composition, identity and readability before detailed
technical findings. Name a few root causes with observed evidence, user impact
and concrete corrections. Distinguish functional/accessibility defects from
stylistic preference and code-only suspicions. Do not equate a pixel diff or a
self-assigned score with aesthetic quality.

For a review request, deliver findings. When fixes are authorized, apply coherent
corrections, inspect affected states and stop once acceptance is met and material
issues are resolved. Use the verdicts in [Design Reviewer](../../../agents/design-reviewer.md).
Disclose self-review and unavailable evidence. No extra agent or engine is required.
