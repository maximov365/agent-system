# Visual workflow comparison

This protocol evaluates a workflow change, not a model's self-reported taste.
Keep fixtures, generated assets and evidence outside downstream application code.
No comparison has been performed merely because this document exists.

## Conditions

Freeze the baseline/candidate framework revisions, brief, factual content,
available tools, asset set, runtime, requested model/effort and target devices.
Record observed model and service tier when exposed, otherwise null. Use the same
budget and acceptance in both conditions. Alternate order across repeated pairs.
Do not expose the competing output to the second run or silently give one a
different tool/asset budget. Save elapsed time, usage when observable, meaningful
defects, interventions and actual evidence. Generated asset costs are separate.

Use at least these different product contexts before a general design claim:

| Surface | Frozen task and content | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Workspace | `fixtures/ui-filter/task.txt`: three-item library, search/clear/empty/keyboard | Automated grader at 1440 and 390 px; inspect all four states and keyboard focus |
| Editorial landing | A fictional local exhibition: title, dates, address, six works with supplied images, reading and ticket-information anchors; no invented acclaim or sales | Desktop/mobile reading order, image crops, long title, anchor interaction, truthful content |
| Game menu/HUD | Use a frozen copy of the existing `examples/game-slice`; retain rules, items and copy; improve only visual presentation | Welcome/play/pause/result, touch and keyboard, reduced/normal motion, measured frame times and legibility during play |

The workspace is executable now. Before executing either other brief, freeze its
exact fixture/content/asset revision and specific interaction assertions in the
run record. These rows alone are not equivalent executable benchmarks. Avoid
unrelated product feature development while preparing evaluation fixtures.

## Review

First enforce behavioral acceptance, accessibility targets and content accuracy.
Retain failures in the record rather than dropping them from a visual comparison.
Show same-size captures and normal-speed recordings under neutral A/B labels;
keep the condition mapping separate until review is recorded. Record reviewer
identity/role, independence, viewed artifacts and reasons, including ties.

Compare task clarity, hierarchy/composition, typography, cohesion, imagery and
motion appropriateness, detail, and small-screen usability. Prefer concrete
observations (a primary action is lost below a dense header) to an unexplained
score. Include interaction and performance defects beside aesthetic judgments.
Self-review is useful but must be labelled and is not independent user preference.

Promote a method only with preserved functionality and credible repeated evidence
across the relevant contexts. A single attractive screenshot or faster tiny task
does not establish general design quality or speed. Publish reviewed numeric and
visual summaries; leave synthetic raw transcripts local and exclude private data.
